# 4. Discussion

SEME separates four quantities that generative EEG-drug papers often conflate. In this battery, the clearest incremental finding is constrained versus unconstrained signed concordance: constrained twins reached 10/10 on TUH, OSF, and P-ADIC while matched unconstrained twins failed (3/10–4/10), and fixed-fold multi-seed runs mostly retained 9/10 to 10/10 constrained signs. Continuous external \(r\) is not an architecture-level property: locked values \(0.908/0.851/0.918\) fall to five-fold means near 0.37 to 0.39 and to typical non-42 values of 0.08 to 0.43. Prior-only already reaches 10/10 signs at mean \(r=0.636\), and the fold ensemble (\(0.642/0.626/0.627\)) does not clearly exceed that prior, so the CVAE is not claimed to uniquely explain binary signs or to have independently discovered drug direction. Magnitude diagnosis fails across the escalation sequence, and the classical-versus-latent encoding bake-off retains only partial label information (0.579; best head 0.593) below theta/alpha 0.675 and a 2185-D logistic feature-space reference of 0.699.

**What the CVAE adds under SEME (incremental-value box).** (i) Relative to a matched unconstrained twin, pharmacodynamic penalties buy signed concordance with the training constrained signature on the locked external cohorts. (ii) Relative to prior-only, the locked continuous-\(r\) checkpoint can exceed the prior’s mean \(r=0.636\), but that continuous advantage is checkpoint-specific and is not established as fold- or seed-stable. (iii) Relative to classical CAUEEG feature probes, frozen \(\mu_{\mathrm{base}}\) readouts do not win the encoding bake-off. SEME’s job is to keep those three comparisons separate.

Relative to diagnosis-oriented EEG twins such as DADD [Amato2025DADD, Amato2026DADDnpj], this case study occupies a different niche: ChemBERTa-conditioned simulation under explicit band/connectivity penalties, scored as a measurement framework rather than as a clinically validated pharmacodynamic predictor [CorralAcero2020DigitalTwin]. The dissociation parallels known separations among reconstruction, perturbation fidelity, and predictive utility in VAEs [Kingma2014VAE, Rampasek2019DrVAE]. Practically, the twin is best used for constraint-consistent signature concordance and hypothesis generation. If diagnosis is required, calibrate against feature-level references rather than frozen \(\mu_{\mathrm{base}}\).

**What this study establishes.** SEME as a reusable endpoint taxonomy; feasibility of a literature-constrained CVAE with reproducible implementation details; external sign concordance under the locked protocol; constrained-versus-unconstrained and prior-only/imitation controls on that endpoint; separation of signs, continuous \(r\), magnitude, and encoding; and encoding attenuation under tested readouts relative to feature references.

**What it does not establish.** Empirical post-dose pharmacodynamic validation; independent pharmacological discovery; seed- or fold-stable continuous \(r\); diagnostic superiority; ChemBERTa generalization; an information-theoretic bottleneck; a supervised deep 2185-D classifier; superiority over prior-only on signs; or constraint-caused diagnostic compression (unconstrained latent AUC was not higher than constrained).

Limitations are specific: no paired post-dose EEG; \(N=66\) versus multilayer capacity; freeze documented but not auditable preregistration; linear imitation uses CVAE-simulated targets; empty default ICA exclusion; CAUEEG dementia-label heterogeneity; exploratory \(\alpha_c\) sweep; rematched ChemBERTa/one-hot arms do not recover the locked high-\(r\) regime. Future work includes broader seed\(\times\)fold grids under the same SEME taxonomy and a public tagged release of the regeneration package.
