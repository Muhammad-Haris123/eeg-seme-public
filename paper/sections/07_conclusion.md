# 5. Conclusion

This paper contributes SEME (Signed concordance, Effect-vector fidelity, Magnitude transfer, Encoding retention), a formal evaluation protocol for literature-constrained, chemistry-conditioned EEG generators. On a pharmacodynamically penalized Donepezil/Memantine CVAE case study, constrained twins exceeded unconstrained twins on the locked sign endpoint (10/10 versus 3/10–4/10 on TUH/OSF/P-ADIC), while continuous fidelity at locked fold-0 seed-42 (\(r=0.908/0.851/0.918\)) was fold- and seed-sensitive, prior-only already achieved 10/10 signs at mean \(r=0.636\), magnitude diagnosis did not transfer, and an encoding bake-off left latent probes (0.579; best head OOF 0.593) below feature references (0.675 and 0.699). The findings support a scoped measurement framework for constrained EEG-drug simulation, not a clinically validated pharmacodynamic digital twin, not seed-stable continuous external fidelity, and not standalone external diagnosis.
