# 1. Introduction

Chemistry-conditioned generative models are increasingly used to simulate how a named intervention would change physiological features. For such models to be scientifically usable, several endpoints must be scored separately: signed response-direction concordance with a constrained training signature, stability of continuous effect-vector fidelity across folds and seeds, diagnostic magnitude transfer, residual diagnostic information after encoding, and incremental value beyond a literature prior. Conflating those questions invites a common failure mode in which a model looks useful on one metric while another fails. Conditional variational autoencoders (CVAEs) make that risk concrete, because reconstruction, perturbation fidelity, and downstream classification need not move together [Kingma2014VAE, Higgins2017betaVAE, Rampasek2019DrVAE, Lotfollahi2019scGen]. What remains scarce is a formal evaluation design that measures those quantities on the same EEG-drug generator and localizes dissociation when they diverge.

Closest generative methods address parts of that problem. A standard CVAE conditions a Gaussian decoder without a signed pharmacodynamic penalty [Kingma2014VAE]. A \(\beta\)-VAE reweights KL for disentanglement rather than drug-direction priors [Higgins2017betaVAE]. Dr.VAE and scGen model molecular or single-cell perturbation response [Rampasek2019DrVAE, Lotfollahi2019scGen]. EEG digital-twin work such as DADD targets diagnosis and prognosis rather than chemistry-conditioned response concordance [Amato2025DADD, Amato2026DADDnpj]. Alzheimer's disease (AD) drug response is a natural stress test: acetylcholinesterase inhibitors help only a subset of patients [Lu2020DonepezilGenetics, Pozzi2022AChEIPredictors], and EEG slowing and connectivity changes are established AD markers with detectable cholinergic effects [Jeong2004EEGreview, Cassani2018EEGADReview, Babiloni2006DonepezilEEG]. Those facts motivate constrained in silico simulation of Donepezil and Memantine. They do not license treating every latent as a clinical biomarker or every signature match as post-dose validation.

This paper contributes **SEME** (Signed concordance, Effect-vector fidelity, Magnitude transfer, Encoding retention): a formal evaluation protocol for literature-constrained, chemistry-conditioned EEG generators. The worked example is a pharmacodynamically penalized CVAE that maps 2185-dimensional resting-state EEG features and frozen ChemBERTa embeddings for Donepezil and Memantine into latent space for in silico post-drug feature simulation [Chithrananda2020ChemBERTa, Winblad2007Memantine]. Under SEME we score signed concordance and continuous effect-vector correlation on Temple University Hospital (TUH) EEG [Obeid2016TUH], an Open Science Framework (OSF) AD/healthy control set, and P-ADIC AD/healthy control recordings [Shor2021PADIC]; magnitude diagnosis across an escalating sequence that adds CAUEEG Normal/MCI/Dementia strata [Kim2023CAUEEG]; and CAUEEG encoding probes against spectral and raw-feature references. Training features use OpenNeuro ds004504 [Miltiadous2023OpenNeuro]. Domain shift is treated as part of the test [Gulrajani2021DomainShift].

The paper does not claim clinical validation, pharmacological efficacy, post-dose digital-twin validation, diagnostic superiority, ChemBERTa chemical generalization, or constraint-caused diagnostic compression. The contributions are:

- SEME as a decoupled protocol that treats signs, continuous \(r\), magnitude, and encoding as separate endpoints, with matched unconstrained, prior-only (circularity budget), linear imitation, five-fold, and multi-seed controls as core method rather than afterthought.
- On that protocol, constrained twins exceed unconstrained twins on the locked sign endpoint (10/10 versus 3/10–4/10 on TUH/OSF/P-ADIC), while continuous \(r\) at locked fold-0 seed-42 (\(0.908/0.851/0.918\)) is fold- and seed-sensitive (five-fold means \(0.385/0.371/0.372\); non-42 seeds typically 0.08 to 0.43) and prior-only already reaches 10/10 signs at mean \(r = 0.636\).
- Magnitude does not diagnose reliably across four escalating external tests, and a classical-versus-latent encoding bake-off retains only partial label information in \(\mu_{\mathrm{base}}\) (latent ROC-AUC 0.579; best head OOF 0.593) below theta/alpha 0.675 and a 2185-D logistic feature-space reference of 0.699.
